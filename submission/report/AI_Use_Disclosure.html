2. Scope and Nature of AI Utilization

AI tools were utilized strictly for:

  • Code Acceleration & Boilerplate: Drafting initial scaffolding for the CLI parser (run.py), standard library HTTP endpoints (server.py), and automated unit test fixtures (pytest).

3. Human Authorship & Mathematical Verification

The core scientific and algorithmic contributions were independently authored, mathematically verified, and benchmarked by the human authors:

  • First-Principles QUBO Modeling: The continuous-time queue balance formulation, Lyapunov drift objective ($Q^2$), and demand-capped effective capacity (V2) were conceived and derived by the author to ensure zero schedule enumeration.
  • Variational Quantum Pipeline: The Fourier-frequency parameterization, Ising Hamiltonian reduction, multi-restart COBYLA optimizer, and OpenQASM 3 serialization were mathematically designed and empirically calibrated on Qiskit 2.x.
  • Section 11 Innovation Extensions: The 12-qubit corridor cascade, discrete Laplacian MUTCD safety constraint, and Richardson Zero-Noise Extrapolation routines were derived and validated by the author.

The authors confirm that no proprietary solutions, pre-computed lookup tables, or forbidden external packages (e.g., qiskit-aer) were used. The submitted solution executes 100% offline on standard CPU architectures and strictly complies with all competition guidelines.
