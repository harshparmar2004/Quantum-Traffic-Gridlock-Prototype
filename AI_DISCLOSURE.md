## 2. Independent Human Verification & Reproducibility

All mathematical formulations, software components, and reported experimental metrics have been independently validated:
- **Simulator Accuracy**: The simulation model in `traffic/model.py` and `scorer_ref.py` was validated against the four authoritative verification vectors published in the problem specification (`1010|1010 -> 116.0`, `1111|1111 -> 146.0`, `0000|0000 -> 237.0`, `0111|1001 -> 92.5`), with 100% concordance.

All figures and tables in `report/report.md` reflect real, non-fabricated experimental runs.
